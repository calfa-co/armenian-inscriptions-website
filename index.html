Machine values are preserved. Corrections made by specialists are stored alongside them, with contributor and date. Records not yet checked may contain errors; the printed page is authoritative.

Editorial signs

Transcriptions are diplomatic and reproduce the signs of the printed edition. Their exact use varies between volumes; each volume's table of conventional signs is authoritative.
