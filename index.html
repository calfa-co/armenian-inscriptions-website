How records are built

Each notice starts as the machine extraction, which is never edited. A published correction replaces individual fields; any field not corrected keeps its machine value. Corrections are read live from the data repository, so an export reflects them within minutes of being accepted. Corrections you have not yet sent are never included.

Method and data quality

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Records were extracted automatically by a vision-language model from scans of the printed volumes; facsimile drawings were located by an object detector. Aenean eu leo quam. Pellentesque ornare sem lacinia quam venenatis vestibulum.

Machine values are preserved. Corrections made by specialists are stored alongside them, with contributor and date. Records not yet checked may contain errors; the printed page is authoritative.

Editorial signs

Transcriptions are diplomatic and reproduce the signs of the printed edition. Their exact use varies between volumes; each volume's table of conventional signs is authoritative.

    Proofreading

    Corrections are made by specialists who read Armenian epigraphic script and know the Divan's conventions. Anyone may report a problem from a notice's menu.

    How corrections work

    1. EditOpen a notice and press Edit. Changes stay in this browser.
    2. SendReview the change and open it as a GitHub issue. Nothing is sent until you press Create on GitHub.
    3. RecordedAn automatic check validates and commits it under your name. The machine reading is kept.
