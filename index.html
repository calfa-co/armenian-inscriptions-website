How this works

  1. Edit what is wrong Every field is editable, and you can move or redraw the box around the inscription. Nothing is sent while you do this — your changes stay in this browser.
  2. Send when you are ready Press Send to GitHub. You are shown exactly what will go, then a pre-filled GitHub issue opens. Nothing leaves your browser until you press Create there. If you have no GitHub account it will ask you to make one — that is how you get credited.
  3. It is checked and recorded An automatic check validates the correction — brackets must balance, a box must fall on the page — then commits it with your name on it. It appears here within a few minutes. The machine's original reading is kept beside yours, never overwritten.
